Sesame Miso Cookies

A delightful butter cookie recipe for the summer months, with a twist! My friend Hyeok gave me some high-quality Korean sesame seeds, which led me to this recipe.

Preparation time: 15 minutes (+1 hour wait) Bake time: 15 minutes

Ingredients
2/3 cup all-purpose flour
1/4 cup sugar
1/2 teaspoon baking powder
1/4 cup cold butter, diced
1 Tablespoon white miso paste (I use this one)
1 egg yolk
2 Tablespoons toasted sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Blitz flour, sugar and baking powder in a food processor
  2. Pulse in the cold butter, miso and egg yolk; you are done when the dough forms a ball that rolls on top of your food processor blade
  3. Take dough ball and shape into a log (~6 inches long)
  4. Wrap in parchment paper, twist ends to seal
  5. Refrigerate for 1 hour
  6. (go read a book or take a walk)
  7. Preheat oven to 350°F
  8. Take log out of oven and unwrap, roll it in sesame seeds to coat
  9. Cut into 16 discs, and place on parchment-lined baking sheet
  10. Bake for 15 minutes, or until edges just start to brown
  11. Remove from oven and set aside; after 10 minutes, transfer to a cooling rack

Once the cookies have cooled completely, enjoy!
