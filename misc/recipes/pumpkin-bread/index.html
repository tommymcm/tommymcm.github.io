Pumpkin Bread

Pumpkin spice season beckons, and my Mom always answered the call with this simple quick bread recipe.

Preparation time: 15 minutes
Bake time: ~75 minutes

Ingredients
2 cups all-purpose flour
1/2 teaspoon salt
1/2 teaspoon baking powder
1 teaspoon baking soda
1 teaspoon ground cloves
1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
2 cups sugar
3/4 cup butter (softened)
2 eggs
16oz can pumpkin puree

Steps

Preparation:

  1. Lightly grease a bread pan
  2. Preheat oven to 325°F

Wet ingredients:

  1. Cream butter and sugar together
  2. Add eggs to mixture, and beat until fully incorporated
  3. Add pumpkin to mixture, and mix until fully incorporated

Combine:

  1. Mix all dry ingredients together until well combined
  2. Add wet mixture
  3. Stir until well incorporated (i.e., when you can't see separate dry ingredients, this doubles as your forearm workout for the day)

Bake:

  1. Bake at 325°F for 75 minutes, or until a chopstick comes out clean

Slice and enjoy!

Modifications

This recipe can be easily modified for other fruits and vegetables. Just replace the pumpkin puree with an equal amount of apple/pear sauce, etc.
