Chocolate Zucchini Bread

When you're craving something sweet, but want to satisfy a healthy conscience, look no further than zucchini bread. When you don't care about the healthy part, add some chocolate!

Preparation time: 15 minutes
Bake time: ~60 minutes

Ingredients
1 cup all-purpose flour
½ cup cocoa powder
½ teaspoon salt
1 teaspoon baking soda
½ cup butter (softened)
¾ cup brown sugar
2 eggs
½ cup olive oil
1 teaspoon (pure) vanilla extract
1½ cup shredded zucchini (~1 squash)
1 cup dark chocolate chips, divided

Steps

Preparation:

  1. Lightly grease a bread pan
  2. Preheat oven to 350°F
  3. Shred zucchini, I use the large size on a box grater

Wet ingredients:

  1. Cream butter and brown sugar together
  2. Add eggs to mixture, and beat until fully incorporated
  3. Add olive oil and vanilla extract, mix until combined

Combine:

  1. Mix all dry ingredients together until well combined
  2. Add wet mixture
  3. Stir until well incorporated (i.e., when you can't see separate dry ingredients, this doubles as your forearm workout for the day)
  4. Add zucchini and ½ cup of chocolate chips, and stir until well incorporated

Bake:

  1. Turn batter out into bread pan
  2. (Optional) Add chocolate chips on top
  3. Bake at 350°F for 55-60 minutes, or until a chopstick comes out clean

Slice and enjoy!
